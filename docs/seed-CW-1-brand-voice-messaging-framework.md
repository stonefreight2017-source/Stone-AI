# CW-1: Stone AI Brand Voice & Messaging Framework

## Purpose
Define the exact personality, tone, vocabulary, and energy level that every piece of Stone AI copy must embody. This seed is the single source of truth for how Stone AI sounds across all touchpoints -- marketing, support, onboarding, error messages, and internal communications. Any copywriter, agent, or contributor producing text for Stone AI references this document first.

## Brand Personality

### Core Identity
Stone AI is the sharp, confident operator who already built the thing you're still Googling. We speak like a founder who's been in the trenches -- direct, pragmatic, zero filler. We respect the reader's time because we know what it's worth.

### Personality Traits
- **Direct**: We say what we mean in the fewest words possible. No hedging.
- **Confident, not arrogant**: We know what we built. We don't need to shout about it.
- **Pragmatic**: Every sentence either informs or moves someone to act. No decorative copy.
- **Warm when it counts**: Support and Bestie contexts get human warmth. Marketing stays sharp.
- **Speed-obsessed**: Our copy mirrors our product -- fast, clean, no wasted cycles.

### The Voice Test
Stone AI sounds like **a founder who respects your time**, not like **a marketing department that bills by the word**.

Stone AI sounds like **the smartest person at the table who still listens**, not like **a corporate FAQ written by committee**.

Stone AI sounds like **a text from a trusted advisor at 11pm**, not like **a quarterly earnings call transcript**.

## Framework / Standards

### Tone Spectrum by Context

| Context | Energy Level | Tone | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hero / Landing** | High | Bold, declarative, punchy | Conversational-professional | "The workforce of the future is here." |
| **Feature copy** | Medium-high | Confident, benefit-first | Professional-casual | "42 specialists. Zero salaries." |
| **Pricing copy** | Medium | Clear, honest, value-anchored | Professional | "Every tier puts more specialists in your corner." |
| **Onboarding** | Medium | Warm, encouraging, action-oriented | Casual-professional | "You're chatting with 4 AI agents before your coffee gets cold." |
| **Support / Help** | Medium-low | Calm, clear, solution-focused | Professional-warm | "Here's what happened and how to fix it." |
| **Error messages** | Low | Honest, brief, next-step-first | Neutral-professional | "That didn't go through. Try again or contact support." |
| **Bestie (companion)** | Variable | Empathetic, adaptive, personal | Depends on user style | Matches user's chosen communication style |
| **Legal / Security** | Low | Precise, plain-language, trustworthy | Formal-professional | "Your data is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM." |
| **Community / Forum** | Medium | Inclusive, encouraging, peer-level | Casual | "Welcome in. Share what you're building." |
| **Email / Notifications** | Medium-low | Respectful, concise, action-clear | Professional | "Your Builder plan is active. Here's what you can do now." |

### Sentence Structure Rules
1. **Lead with the benefit, not the feature.** "Answers in under 100ms" beats "Our inference engine delivers sub-100ms latency."
2. **Short sentences dominate. Long sentences earn their place.** If a sentence has more than 25 words, it must carry a complete thought that can't be split.
3. **Active voice always.** "Stone AI encrypts your data" not "Your data is encrypted by Stone AI."
4. **Second person by default.** "You choose when" not "Users can choose when."
5. **Concrete over abstract.** "$0 per message" not "cost-effective messaging solution."

### Vocabulary

#### Approved Terms (use these)
| Term | Use For |
|---|---|
| Stone AI | Full brand name (always with trademark on first mention per page) |
| Stone Engine | The local inference engine |
| Smart Mode | Cloud AI mode |
| AI Bestie | The companion feature |
| agents / specialists / thinkers | The 42 AI agents |
| local-first | Our architecture philosophy |
| your data stays yours | Privacy positioning |
| Free / Builder / Growth / Executive / Reseller | Tier display names |
| founding member | Early adopter status |
| OG badge | Visual founding member marker |
| Concept E | Brand insignia name |

#### Banned Phrases (never use these)
| Banned | Why | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "revolutionary" / "groundbreaking" | Overpromise, cliche | "built for [specific outcome]" |
| "best AI on the market" | Unverifiable superlative | "42 specialists, one platform" |
| "unlimited" (for Smart Mode) | Technically false -- daily caps exist | "generous daily allowance" or state the actual cap |
| "guaranteed results" | Legal liability | "designed to deliver [specific thing]" |
| "we're better than [competitor]" | Legal risk, brand damage | Never mention competitors by name |
| Any competitor name | Legal and brand policy | Describe our strengths on their own merit |
| "synergy" / "leverage" / "paradigm" | Corporate jargon | Plain language equivalent |
| "AI-powered" as a standalone value | Meaningless in 2026 | State what the AI actually does |
| "just" / "simply" / "easily" | Minimizes user effort, often untrue | Describe the actual steps |
| "disrupt" / "disruptive" | Overused startup cliche | Describe the actual change we create |
| "world-class" | Unverifiable | State the specific quality metric |
| "military-grade encryption" | Misleading term | "AES-256-GCM encryption" |

### Number & Formatting Rules
- Always use specific numbers: "42 agents" not "dozens of agents"
- Prices always include the dollar sign and cents for paid tiers: "$19.99" not "20 dollars"
- Free tier: "$0" not "free" in price displays (use "Free" as the tier name, "$0" as the price)
- Response times: "<100ms" with the less-than symbol
- Use "per day" not "/day" in body copy (slashes OK in UI labels and technical specs)

## Templates & Examples

### Hero Headline Formula
**[Bold declarative statement] + [Subhead that grounds it in specifics]**

Good: "The workforce of the future is here." + "42 AI specialists that think so you don't have to."
Good: "Two engines. You choose." + "Local mode is fast, free, and private. Smart mode brings cloud AI when you need it."
Bad: "Welcome to the Future of AI-Powered Business Solutions" (jargon, passive, generic)

### Feature Copy Formula
**[What it does in human terms] + [Why that matters to you]**

Good: "Answers in under 100ms -- faster than you can type the question"
Good: "Your financials, client names, and strategy stay on our servers -- never shared"
Bad: "Leveraging cutting-edge inference optimization for enterprise-grade response latency"

### CTA Copy
- Primary: "Start for Free" / "Get Started" / "Start Free"
- Secondary: "See Plans" / "Compare Tiers"
- Promo: "Claim $9.99 Deal" / "Claim $14.99 Deal" / "Claim $39.99 Deal"
- Enterprise: "Build Your Plan" / "Contact Sales"
- Never: "Learn More" (too passive), "Submit" (too clinical), "Buy Now" (too aggressive for SaaS)

### Urgency / Scarcity (honest only)
- OK: "One-time offer -- price locked forever. If you cancel, this deal is gone."
- OK: "Founding members earn the OG badge."
- NOT OK: "Only 3 spots left!" (unless literally true and verifiable)
- NOT OK: "This offer expires in 24 hours!" (unless it actually does)

## DO / DON'T Rules

### DO
- Lead every section with the reader's benefit
- Use real numbers, real caps, real prices -- transparency builds trust
- Mirror the product's speed in the copy's pace -- short, clean, fast
- Vary sentence length for rhythm but bias toward short
- Use em dashes for emphasis and parenthetical asides
- Write like the reader is already smart -- don't explain obvious things
- Test every headline by asking: "Would a busy founder read past this?"

### DON'T
- Never name a competitor in any copy, ad, or marketing material
- Never make claims about performance we can't prove with current production metrics
- Never use "unlimited" for anything that has a cap (Smart Mode, tokens, etc.)
- Never write a paragraph where a bullet list would be clearer
- Never use passive voice in headlines or CTAs
- Never start a sentence with "We believe" or "Our mission is" in marketing copy (OK on About page)
- Never use exclamation marks in body copy (OK sparingly in promos, max 1 per section)
- Never write "click here" -- describe the destination instead
- Never assume the reader knows what "local-first" means -- always provide the one-line explanation on first use per page
