## ARTICLE 9 — TAX MATTERS

### 9.1 S-Corporation Election

The Company has made an election to be treated as an S-Corporation for federal income tax purposes by filing IRS Form 2553 with the Internal Revenue Service. This election is in effect as of the effective date of this Agreement.

### 9.2 Tax Treatment

Consistent with the S-Corporation election:

(a) The Company shall file IRS Form 1120-S (U.S. Income Tax Return for an S Corporation) annually;

(b) The Member shall receive Schedule K-1 reflecting the Member's share of income, deductions, and credits;

(c) The Member shall report all pass-through income on the Member's individual federal income tax return (IRS Form 1040);

(d) The Member shall receive reasonable compensation as a W-2 employee of the Company (see Article 5.1);

(e) Remaining profits may be distributed as shareholder distributions, not subject to self-employment tax, consistent with IRS regulations for S-Corporations.

### 9.3 Tax Filings

The Member, or the Company's designated tax advisor or preparer, shall prepare and timely file all required federal, state, and local tax returns on behalf of the Company. This includes, but is not limited to:

(a) Federal Form 1120-S;

(b) New York State corporate tax returns (Form CT-3-S or as otherwise required);

(c) Any required New York City or local tax filings;

(d) Quarterly payroll tax returns (Forms 941);

(e) Annual wage reporting (Forms W-2, W-3);

(f) Any required 1099 forms for independent contractors.

### 9.4 Tax Elections

The Member shall have the authority to make all tax elections on behalf of the Company, including but not limited to elections under Sections 754, 179, and 168(k) of the Internal Revenue Code.

### 9.5 Tax Reserves

The Company shall maintain reasonable reserves for estimated tax obligations, including quarterly estimated tax payments for both the Company and the Member's individual tax liability arising from Company income.

## ARTICLE 10 — DISSOLUTION

### 10.1 Events of Dissolution

The Company shall be dissolved upon the earliest to occur of the following:

(a) The written decision of the Member to dissolve the Company;

(b) The entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under Section 702 of the NY LLCL;

(c) The occurrence of any event that makes it unlawful for the Company to continue its business;

(d) Upon the death, disability, or incapacity of the Member, ONLY if the Successor Manager designated in Article 7 is unable or unwilling to serve and no alternate successor has been designated.

### 10.2 Winding Up

Upon dissolution, the Company's affairs shall be wound up by the Member (or the Successor Manager, if applicable). The winding up shall include:

(a) Collection of all Company assets;

(b) Payment or provision for payment of all Company debts, liabilities, and obligations in the order of priority required by law;

(c) Distribution of remaining assets to the Member;

(d) Filing of Articles of Dissolution with the New York Department of State;

(e) Cancellation or transfer of all domain names, trademarks, and other registered assets;

(f) Notification to all customers, vendors, and service providers;

(g) Proper archival or destruction of customer data in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

### 10.3 Final Tax Returns

Upon dissolution, the Company shall file all final tax returns and comply with all applicable tax requirements, including notification to the IRS of the termination of the S-Corporation election.
