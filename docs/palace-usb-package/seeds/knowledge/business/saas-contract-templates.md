## 1. Executive Summary

As a SaaS business grows beyond individual consumers to enterprise customers, partners, vendors, and contractors, the volume and complexity of contractual relationships increases dramatically. Each relationship type requires a tailored agreement that protects the company's interests while meeting the other party's expectations and legal requirements.

This seed provides production-ready templates and frameworks for the six most critical contract types: Enterprise License Agreements (ELA), Service Level Agreements (SLA), Data Processing Agreements (DPA), Vendor Contracts, Contractor Agreements, and Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).

## 2. Enterprise License Agreement (ELA)

### 2.1 When You Need an ELA

An ELA is required when selling to business customers who need:
- Multiple user seats under one account
- Custom terms beyond standard ToS
- SLA commitments
- Data processing guarantees
- Volume pricing
- Custom integrations or features

### 2.2 ELA Structure

```
ENTERPRISE LICENSE AGREEMENT

Between:
Licensor: [Company Name] LLC ("Provider")
Licensee: [Enterprise Customer] ("Customer")

Effective Date: [Date]
Term: [12/24/36 months]

1. DEFINITIONS
   1.1 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents
       authorized to use the Service under this Agreement.
   1.2 "Service" means the [Stone AI / Stone AI Tools] platform, including all
       features, updates, and documentation provided during the Term.
   1.3 "Customer Data" means all data uploaded, submitted, or generated by
       Customer or its Authorized Users through the Service.
   1.4 "Documentation" means user guides, API documentation, and other materials
       describing the Service's functionality.
   1.5 "Order Form" means the document specifying the Service tier, number of
       Authorized Users, fees, and term, attached as Exhibit A.

2. LICENSE GRANT
   2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement and payment of applicable fees,
       Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited license
       to access and use the Service during the Term for Customer's internal
       business purposes.
   2.2 The license is limited to the number of Authorized Users specified in the
       Order Form.
   2.3 Customer may not sublicense, resell, or distribute access to the Service.

3. SERVICE DESCRIPTION
   3.1 Provider shall make the Service available in accordance with the Service
       Level Agreement attached as Exhibit B.
   3.2 Provider may update the Service from time to time, provided that such
       updates do not materially reduce the functionality available to Customer.
   3.3 Provider shall provide support as described in Exhibit C (Support Terms).

4. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS
   4.1 Customer shall ensure Authorized Users comply with the Acceptable Use Policy.
   4.2 Customer is responsible for the security of account credentials.
   4.3 Customer shall not attempt to reverse engineer or access source code.
   4.4 Customer shall comply with all applicable laws in its use of the Service.

5. FEES AND PAYMENT
   5.1 Customer shall pay the fees specified in the Order Form.
   5.2 Fees are due [net 30 / net 45] days from invoice date.
   5.3 Late payments accrue interest at [1.5%] per month or the maximum rate
       permitted by law, whichever is less.
   5.4 All fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is responsible for applicable
       taxes, excluding Provider's income taxes.
   5.5 Fees for renewal terms may increase by up to [5%] with [60 days'] notice.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   6.1 Provider retains all rights in the Service, including software, AI models,
       algorithms, and documentation.
   6.2 Customer retains all rights in Customer Data.
   6.3 Customer grants Provider a limited license to use Customer Data solely to
       provide and improve the Service.
   6.4 Provider shall not use Customer Data to train AI models available to other
       customers without Customer's written consent.

7. DATA PROTECTION
   7.1 Provider shall process Customer Data in accordance with the Data Processing
       Agreement attached as Exhibit D.
   7.2 Provider implements security measures as described in the Security Addendum.
   7.3 Provider shall notify Customer of any data breach affecting Customer Data
       within [24/48/72] hours of discovery.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
   8.1 Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with
       reasonable care.
   8.2 Confidential Information shall not be disclosed to third parties except
       as necessary to perform under this Agreement.
   8.3 Confidentiality obligations survive termination for [3 years].

9. WARRANTIES
   9.1 Provider warrants that:
       (a) The Service will perform materially as described in the Documentation
       (b) The Service will comply with the SLA
       (c) Provider has the right to grant the license herein
       (d) The Service will not, to Provider's knowledge, infringe third-party IP
   9.2 DISCLAIMER: EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS."
       PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
    10.1 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL,
         CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.
    10.2 EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER
         IN THE [12] MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.
    10.3 The limitations in 10.1 and 10.2 do not apply to:
         (a) Breach of confidentiality obligations
         (b) Intellectual property infringement
         (c) Customer's payment obligations
         (d) Willful misconduct or gross negligence

11. INDEMNIFICATION
    11.1 Provider shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the
         Service infringes intellectual property rights, provided Customer
         promptly notifies Provider, grants Provider control of the defense,
         and cooperates.
    11.2 Customer shall indemnify Provider against claims arising from Customer's
         use of the Service in violation of this Agreement or applicable law.

12. TERM AND TERMINATION
    12.1 The initial Term is specified in the Order Form.
    12.2 The Agreement automatically renews for successive [12-month] periods
         unless either party provides [60 days'] written notice of non-renewal.
    12.3 Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains
         uncured [30 days] after written notice.
    12.4 Provider may terminate immediately if Customer fails to pay fees after
         [15 days'] notice of overdue payment.
    12.5 Upon termination:
         (a) Customer's access to the Service ceases
         (b) Provider shall make Customer Data available for export for [30 days]
         (c) After the export period, Provider shall delete Customer Data
         (d) Surviving provisions: IP, Confidentiality, Limitation of Liability,
             Indemnification, Dispute Resolution

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
    13.1 Governed by the laws of the State of New York.
    13.2 Disputes shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation for [30 days].
    13.3 Unresolved disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration under AAA
         Commercial Rules in New York, NY.
    13.4 Either party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent
         jurisdiction.

14. GENERAL PROVISIONS
    14.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement, including Exhibits, constitutes the
         entire agreement between the parties.
    14.2 Amendments: Must be in writing signed by both parties.
    14.3 Assignment: Neither party may assign without written consent, except in
         connection with a merger or acquisition.
    14.4 Force Majeure: Neither party liable for delays due to events beyond
         reasonable control.
    14.5 Severability: Invalid provisions shall be modified to the minimum extent
         necessary.
    14.6 Notices: Written notices to addresses specified in the Order Form.

EXHIBITS:
A. Order Form
B. Service Level Agreement
C. Support Terms
D. Data Processing Agreement

SIGNATURES:

Provider: _________________ Date: _________
Customer: _________________ Date: _________
```

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

### 3.1 SLA Template

```
SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

This SLA is incorporated into the Agreement between Provider and Customer.

1. UPTIME COMMITMENT
   1.1 Provider commits to [99.9%] monthly uptime for the Service ("Uptime SLA").
   1.2 Uptime is calculated as:
       (Total minutes in month - Downtime minutes) / Total minutes in month × 100

   1.3 "Downtime" means the Service is unavailable to Customer, excluding:
       (a) Scheduled maintenance (with [48 hours'] notice)
       (b) Emergency maintenance (reasonable efforts to notify)
       (c) Force majeure events
       (d) Customer's internet connectivity issues
       (e) Third-party service outages outside Provider's control
       (f) Issues caused by Customer's actions or configurations

2. SERVICE CREDITS
   If Provider fails to meet the Uptime SLA:

   | Monthly Uptime | Service Credit |
   |---------------|---------------|
   | 99.0% - 99.9% | 10% of monthly fee |
   | 95.0% - 99.0% | 25% of monthly fee |
   | Below 95.0% | 50% of monthly fee |

   2.1 Service credits are applied to future invoices, not refunded as cash.
   2.2 Maximum service credit per month: 50% of monthly fee.
   2.3 Customer must request credits within [30 days] of the downtime event.
   2.4 Service credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for downtime.

3. SUPPORT LEVELS

   | Priority | Description | Response Time | Resolution Target |
   |----------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
   | P1 Critical | Service completely unavailable | 1 hour | 4 hours |
   | P2 High | Major feature unavailable, no workaround | 4 hours | 8 hours |
   | P3 Medium | Feature impaired, workaround available | 8 business hours | 3 business days |
   | P4 Low | Minor issue, cosmetic, feature request | 2 business days | Best effort |

   3.1 Business hours: 9 AM — 6 PM Eastern Time, Monday-Friday (excluding
       US federal holidays).
   3.2 P1 support is available 24/7 for [Pro/Enterprise] tier customers.

4. MAINTENANCE WINDOWS
   4.1 Scheduled maintenance: Sundays 2:00-6:00 AM ET (with 48 hours' notice)
   4.2 Emergency maintenance: As needed with best-effort advance notice
   4.3 Maintenance will not exceed [4 hours] per month (excluding emergencies)

5. PERFORMANCE METRICS
   5.1 API response time: 95th percentile < [500ms] (excluding AI generation)
   5.2 AI generation response time: 95th percentile < [10 seconds]
   5.3 Page load time: < [3 seconds] for initial load
   5.4 Provider shall provide monthly performance reports upon request.

6. MONITORING AND REPORTING
   6.1 Provider maintains uptime monitoring at [monitoring URL]
   6.2 Status page available at [status page URL]
   6.3 Customer will be notified of incidents via email within [15 minutes]
   6.4 Post-incident reports provided within [5 business days] for P1/P2 incidents
```

## 4. Vendor Contract Template

### 4.1 Vendor Services Agreement

```
VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between:
Client: [Company Name] LLC ("Client")
Vendor: [Vendor Name] ("Vendor")

1. SERVICES
   Vendor shall provide the following services: [Description]

2. TERM
   Start date: [Date]
   End date: [Date] or [month-to-month with 30 days' notice]

3. FEES
   3.1 Vendor shall invoice [monthly/quarterly] at rates specified in Exhibit A.
   3.2 Payment terms: Net [30] days from invoice date.
   3.3 Expenses: Pre-approved expenses reimbursed at cost with receipts.

4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
   4.1 Vendor represents that services will be performed in a professional manner.
   4.2 Vendor represents compliance with all applicable laws.
   4.3 Vendor represents it has the right and authority to provide the services.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   5.1 All work product created by Vendor in the course of services is owned
       by Client (work for hire or assigned).
   5.2 Vendor retains ownership of pre-existing IP, granting Client a perpetual
       license to use pre-existing IP incorporated into deliverables.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
   6.1 Vendor shall maintain confidentiality of all Client information.
   6.2 Vendor shall not disclose Client information to third parties.
   6.3 Obligations survive termination for [3 years].

7. DATA PROTECTION
   7.1 If Vendor processes personal data, the DPA in Exhibit B applies.
   7.2 Vendor shall implement reasonable security measures.
   7.3 Vendor shall notify Client of any data breach within [24 hours].

8. INDEMNIFICATION
   Vendor shall indemnify Client against claims arising from Vendor's breach
   of this Agreement, negligence, or violation of law.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
   Vendor's total liability shall not exceed fees paid in the preceding
   [12 months]. Excludes: IP infringement, confidentiality breach, gross
   negligence.

10. TERMINATION
    10.1 Either party may terminate for convenience with [30 days'] written notice.
    10.2 Either party may terminate for material breach after [15 days'] cure period.
    10.3 Client may terminate immediately if Vendor breaches confidentiality or
         data protection obligations.

11. GOVERNING LAW
    New York State law. Disputes resolved by arbitration in New York, NY.
```

## 5. Independent Contractor Agreement

### 5.1 Contractor Agreement Template

```
INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

Between:
Company: [Company Name] LLC ("Company")
Contractor: [Contractor Name] ("Contractor")

Effective Date: [Date]

1. ENGAGEMENT
   1.1 Company engages Contractor to perform services as described in
       Exhibit A ("Statement of Work").
   1.2 Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee.

2. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES
   2.1 Contractor shall perform services described in the Statement of Work.
   2.2 Contractor shall meet deadlines specified in the Statement of Work.
   2.3 Contractor shall perform services in a professional and workmanlike manner.

3. COMPENSATION
   3.1 Company shall pay Contractor: [Rate — hourly/project/milestone]
   3.2 Payment schedule: [Weekly/biweekly/monthly/upon milestone completion]
   3.3 Invoicing: Contractor shall submit invoices with detailed time/work records.
   3.4 Payment terms: Net [15/30] days from invoice receipt.
   3.5 Contractor is responsible for all taxes. Company will issue Form 1099-NEC
       if payments exceed $600 in a calendar year.

4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS
   4.1 Contractor is NOT an employee of Company. Contractor:
       (a) Controls the manner and means of performing services
       (b) Sets own schedule (subject to reasonable deadlines)
       (c) Provides own equipment and tools (unless otherwise agreed)
       (d) May work for other clients simultaneously
       (e) Is not entitled to employee benefits
       (f) Is responsible for own taxes, insurance, and expenses
   4.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship,
       partnership, joint venture, or agency.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   5.1 ASSIGNMENT: Contractor assigns to Company all right, title, and interest
       in all Work Product created under this Agreement. "Work Product" includes
       all code, designs, documentation, inventions, and other creative works.
   5.2 WORK FOR HIRE: To the extent permitted by law, all Work Product is "work
       made for hire" under the Copyright Act.
   5.3 MORAL RIGHTS: Contractor waives all moral rights in Work Product.
   5.4 PRIOR INVENTIONS: Listed in Exhibit B. Will not be incorporated without
       written consent.
   5.5 FURTHER ASSURANCE: Contractor shall execute any documents necessary to
       perfect Company's ownership.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
   6.1 Contractor shall maintain strict confidentiality of all Company information.
   6.2 "Confidential Information" includes but is not limited to: source code,
       system prompts, AI model configurations, business strategies, user data,
       financial information, and any information marked confidential.
   6.3 Contractor shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose other
       than performing services under this Agreement.
   6.4 Obligations survive termination indefinitely for trade secrets, [3 years]
       for other Confidential Information.

7. NON-SOLICITATION
   7.1 During the term and for [12 months] after termination, Contractor shall
       not directly solicit Company's customers or other contractors for a
       competing business.

8. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
   8.1 Contractor represents that:
       (a) Contractor has the skills and experience to perform the services
       (b) Work Product will be original and will not infringe third-party rights
       (c) Contractor is authorized to enter this Agreement
       (d) Performance will not violate any other agreement

9. TERMINATION
   9.1 Either party may terminate with [14 days'] written notice.
   9.2 Company may terminate immediately for Contractor's breach of
       confidentiality, IP, or material breach.
   9.3 Upon termination:
       (a) Contractor delivers all Work Product and Company materials
       (b) Company pays for services performed through termination date
       (c) Surviving provisions: IP, Confidentiality, Non-Solicitation

10. INDEMNIFICATION
    Contractor shall indemnify Company against claims arising from Contractor's
    breach of this Agreement or infringement of third-party rights.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
    Company's total liability shall not exceed total fees paid under this Agreement.

12. GOVERNING LAW
    New York State law. Disputes resolved by binding arbitration.

13. GENERAL PROVISIONS
    13.1 Entire Agreement
    13.2 Amendments in writing only
    13.3 Severability
    13.4 No waiver unless written

EXHIBITS:
A. Statement of Work
B. Prior Inventions
C. Equipment/Access Provided (if any)

SIGNATURES:

Company: _________________ Date: _________
Contractor: _________________ Date: _________
```

## 6. NDA Template (Comprehensive)

### 6.1 Mutual NDA for Business Discussions

```
MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Between:
Party A: [Company Name] LLC
Party B: [Other Party Name]

Effective Date: [Date]

1. PURPOSE
   The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship regarding
   [describe purpose — partnership, investment, vendor evaluation, etc.]
   ("Purpose") and may need to share confidential information.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
   "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in any form,
   disclosed by either party, including:
   (a) Technical: source code, algorithms, system architecture, AI models,
       system prompts, APIs, databases, security measures
   (b) Business: strategies, financial data, pricing, customer lists, marketing
       plans, product roadmaps, partnership terms
   (c) Legal: contracts, legal proceedings, regulatory filings
   (d) Any information marked "Confidential," "Proprietary," or similar
   (e) Any information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

3. EXCLUSIONS
   Confidential Information does not include information that:
   (a) Is publicly available without breach
   (b) Was already known to the receiving party (with evidence)
   (c) Is independently developed (with evidence)
   (d) Is received from a third party without restriction
   (e) Is required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to discloser)

4. OBLIGATIONS
   Each party shall:
   (a) Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose
   (b) Protect it with at least the same care as its own (minimum: reasonable care)
   (c) Limit disclosure to employees/advisors with need to know who are bound by
       similar obligations
   (d) Not reverse engineer any product or technology disclosed
   (e) Not copy or reproduce except as necessary for the Purpose

5. TERM AND SURVIVAL
   5.1 This Agreement is effective for [2 years] from the Effective Date.
   5.2 Obligations regarding Confidential Information survive for [3 years]
       after expiration or termination.
   5.3 Trade secret obligations survive indefinitely.

6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION
   Upon request or termination, each party shall return or destroy all
   Confidential Information and certify destruction within [10 days].

7. NO LICENSE OR OBLIGATION
   7.1 No license to IP is granted by disclosure of Confidential Information.
   7.2 Neither party is obligated to proceed with any business relationship.
   7.3 No warranty regarding accuracy or completeness of Confidential Information.

8. REMEDIES
   Each party acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm. The
   disclosing party may seek injunctive relief without proving actual damages
   or posting a bond.

9. GENERAL
   9.1 Governing law: New York State
   9.2 Assignment: Not without written consent
   9.3 Entire agreement regarding confidentiality
   9.4 Amendments: Written and signed by both parties
   9.5 Severability

SIGNATURES:

Party A: _________________ Date: _________
Party B: _________________ Date: _________
```

## 7. Contract Management Best Practices

### 7.1 Contract Lifecycle

```
1. DRAFTING → Use templates, customize per relationship
2. REVIEW → Legal review for non-standard terms
3. NEGOTIATION → Track redlines, document concessions
4. EXECUTION → Proper signatures, dating, exhibits
5. STORAGE → Secure, organized, searchable repository
6. MONITORING → Track deadlines, renewals, obligations
7. RENEWAL/TERMINATION → Advance notice, renegotiation
```

### 7.2 Contract Storage System

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|------------|
| Location | Secure cloud storage (encrypted) |
| Organization | By type, then by counterparty, then by date |
| Naming | [Type]-[Counterparty]-[Date]-v[X].pdf |
| Access | Restricted to authorized personnel |
| Backup | Regular encrypted backups |
| Retention | Duration of agreement + [7 years] |
| Calendar | Renewal and termination dates tracked |

### 7.3 Key Dates to Track

- Agreement effective date
- Term expiration date
- Renewal notification deadline
- Payment due dates
- SLA review dates
- DPA annual review dates
- Insurance renewal dates
- Non-solicitation/non-compete expiration
