# Influencer & Partnership Strategy — Stone AI Ecosystem

## Executive Overview

Influencer partnerships are the fastest way to borrow trust at scale. When a respected voice in the AI or tech space recommends Stone AI, their audience transfers a portion of the trust they have in that creator to your product. This is fundamentally different from advertising — ads interrupt, influencers recommend. The conversion rates reflect this: influencer-driven traffic converts 3-5x higher than paid ad traffic because the recommendation comes with built-in social proof.

For the Three-Headed Monster ecosystem, influencer strategy must be multi-layered: AI/tech YouTubers and Twitter personalities for Stone AI (consumer), app reviewers and lifestyle creators for Best AI Mobile, and developer influencers and tech bloggers for Stone AI Tools. Each audience trusts different creators, consumes different content formats, and responds to different messaging.

## Influencer Identification

### Influencer Tiers

**Tier 1: Mega Influencers (500K+ followers)**
- Expensive ($5,000-$50,000+ per integration)
- Broad reach but lower engagement rate (1-3%)
- Best for brand awareness, not direct conversion
- Examples: Major AI YouTubers, tech reviewers, Twitter thought leaders with massive followings

**Tier 2: Mid-Tier Influencers (50K-500K followers)**
- Moderate cost ($1,000-$5,000 per integration)
- Good reach with decent engagement (3-6%)
- Best balance of reach and conversion
- Examples: AI-focused YouTubers, SaaS reviewers, productivity creators

**Tier 3: Micro Influencers (10K-50K followers)**
- Affordable ($200-$1,000 per integration)
- High engagement (5-10%)
- Best for conversion and niche targeting
- Examples: Niche AI bloggers, developer content creators, productivity coaches

**Tier 4: Nano Influencers (1K-10K followers)**
- Very affordable ($50-$200 or product exchange)
- Highest engagement (8-15%)
- Best for authentic recommendations and community building
- Examples: Active Twitter/X AI accounts, tech newsletter writers, Discord community leaders

**Recommended Strategy for Stone AI:** Focus 60% of budget on Tier 3 (micro), 30% on Tier 2 (mid-tier), 10% on Tier 4 (nano). Tier 1 is reserved for major launches or when budget allows.

### Influencer Discovery Methods

**Platform-by-Platform Search:**

**YouTube:**
- Search: "AI tools," "AI agents," "best AI apps," "ChatGPT alternative," "productivity tools"
- Filter by upload date (active creators) and view count (engagement)
- Check subscriber-to-view ratio (healthy: 10-30% of subscribers view each video)
- Tools: Social Blade (free), vidIQ, TubeBuddy

**Twitter/X:**
- Search AI and SaaS keywords, look at who's getting engagement
- Check follower count vs. average likes/retweets (engagement rate)
- Look at reply quality (do their followers actually discuss, or just like?)
- Tools: Followerwonk, SparkToro

**LinkedIn:**
- Search for AI thought leaders, SaaS founders, productivity experts
- Check post engagement and comment quality
- Look for people who write long-form LinkedIn articles (high trust)

**Newsletters:**
- Substack: Search AI and tech categories
- Newsletter directories: The Sample, Newsletter Stack
- Sponsorship platforms: Swapstack, Paved

**Podcasts:**
- Search AI and tech podcast charts
- Check episode download numbers (ask directly or use Rephonic)
- Look for podcasts that review or discuss AI tools

### Influencer Evaluation Criteria

Score each potential influencer partner:

| Criteria | Weight | Scoring (1-10) |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| Audience relevance (do their followers match our ICP?) | 25% | Is this AI/tech/productivity audience? |
| Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares / followers) | 20% | >5% excellent, 3-5% good, <3% mediocre |
| Content quality (production value, depth, authenticity) | 15% | Would we be proud to be featured here? |
| Audience size (reach) | 15% | Relative to tier expectations |
| Brand safety (no controversy, aligned values) | 10% | Any red flags in past content? |
| Past sponsor performance (do their sponsored posts perform?) | 10% | Check past sponsorships for engagement |
| Cost efficiency (estimated CPM or CPA) | 5% | Within budget for expected results? |

## Partnership Models

### Sponsored Content

**Dedicated Video/Post:**
The influencer creates a full piece of content about Stone AI.
- Cost: Higher ($2,000-$20,000+ depending on audience size)
- Control: Medium (provide talking points, influencer creates in their style)
- Performance: Highest (100% of content is about your product)

**Integration/Mention:**
Stone AI is mentioned within a broader piece of content (e.g., "top 5 AI tools" or mid-roll sponsor).
- Cost: Lower ($500-$5,000)
- Control: Lower (brief mention within their content)
- Performance: Good for awareness, lower for direct conversion

**Review/Comparison:**
Influencer does an honest review or comparison including Stone AI.
- Cost: Medium (or free if they choose to review independently)
- Control: Lowest (honest review means you can't dictate the outcome)
- Performance: Highest trust (audiences value honest reviews)

### Affiliate Program

Influencers get a unique tracking link and earn commission on conversions.

**Affiliate Commission Structure:**

| Plan | Commission Per Sale | Commission Type |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| STARTER | $10 (50% of first month) | One-time |
| PLUS | $25 (50% of first month) | One-time |
| SMART | $50 (50% of first month) | One-time |
| PRO | $100 (50% of first month) | One-time |
| Any plan (recurring) | 15% of monthly revenue | Recurring for 12 months |

**Affiliate Program Infrastructure:**
- Tracking: Custom affiliate links with UTM parameters + server-side attribution
- Dashboard: Real-time view of clicks, signups, conversions, and earnings
- Payments: Monthly via Stripe Connect (see Affiliate Revenue Tracking seed)
- Terms: 30-day cookie window, minimum $50 payout threshold

### Product Exchange

For nano influencers and smaller creators: provide free access to Stone AI (PRO plan) in exchange for honest content. This costs almost nothing (marginal cost of a free account) but generates authentic reviews.

**Terms:**
- Free PRO account for 6 months (renewable if content continues)
- No requirement to be positive (authenticity is more valuable than praise)
- Request: at least one piece of content per quarter (post, tweet, video, review)
- Bonus: If their content drives 10+ signups, upgrade to paid partnership

### Co-Marketing Partnerships

Partner with complementary (non-competing) products for mutual promotion.

**Potential Co-Marketing Partners:**

| Partner Type | Example | Co-Marketing Idea |
|-------------|---------|-------------------|
| Productivity tools | Notion, Todoist | "Stone AI + [Tool] workflow guide" |
| Writing platforms | Substack, Medium | "AI-assisted writing masterclass" |
| Developer tools | Vercel, Supabase | "Build an AI app with Stone AI Tools + [Platform]" |
| Education platforms | Coursera, Udemy | "AI skills course featuring Stone AI" |
| Hardware companies | Laptop/phone brands | "Best devices for AI productivity" |

**Co-Marketing Principles:**
1. Partner must have complementary (not competing) audience
2. Both sides contribute equally (content, promotion, resources)
3. Clear success metrics agreed in advance
4. No exclusivity (unless significant value warrants it)
5. Cross-promotion in newsletters, social, and in-product

## Campaign Management

### Influencer Campaign Workflow

```
Week 1: Discovery & Outreach
├── Identify 20-30 potential influencers per campaign
├── Score and rank using evaluation criteria
├── Send initial outreach to top 15
└── Follow up after 5 days on non-responses

Week 2: Negotiation & Briefing
├── Call with interested influencers (15 min, product demo)
├── Agree on deliverables, timeline, compensation
├── Send creative brief (talking points, not script)
├── Provide product access and affiliate links
└── Send brand assets (logos, screenshots, approved claims)

Week 3-4: Content Creation
├── Influencer creates content
├── Optional: Review draft for factual accuracy (not for tone/style)
├── Approve and schedule publication date
└── Prepare amplification (social sharing, email mention)

Week 5+: Publication & Tracking
├── Content goes live
├── Track: link clicks, signups, conversions, engagement
├── Amplify: share on Stone AI social channels
├── Engage: respond to comments on influencer's content
└── Report: weekly performance summary

Month End: Analysis & Optimization
├── Calculate CPA and ROAS per influencer
├── Identify top performers for ongoing partnerships
├── Send performance report to influencers
├── Renew top performers, pause underperformers
└── Feed learnings into next campaign
```

## Legal and Compliance

### FTC Disclosure Requirements

All influencer partnerships must comply with FTC guidelines:

1. **Clear disclosure**: Influencer must clearly disclose the partnership ("Sponsored by Stone AI," "#ad," "Paid partnership with Stone AI")
2. **Prominent placement**: Disclosure must be visible without scrolling or expanding (not buried in description)
3. **Platform-specific**: Use platform's built-in sponsorship tools when available (YouTube "includes paid promotion," Instagram "Paid partnership with" tag)
4. **Every piece of content**: Every post, video, story, or tweet about Stone AI must include disclosure — not just the main deliverable

### Contract Template (Key Terms)

```
1. DELIVERABLES: [Specific content to be created]
2. TIMELINE: Content due by [date], published by [date]
3. COMPENSATION: [Amount] paid within [30 days] of publication
4. USAGE RIGHTS: Stone AI may reshare the content on owned channels
   for [12 months] with credit to the creator
5. EXCLUSIVITY: Creator will not promote direct competitors for
   [30 days] before and after publication (optional, costs more)
6. DISCLOSURE: Creator must comply with FTC guidelines
7. APPROVAL: Stone AI may review content for factual accuracy
   (NOT for tone, opinion, or editorial direction)
8. TERMINATION: Either party may cancel with [7 days] notice
   before content creation begins
9. OWNERSHIP: Creator retains ownership of their content
```

### Brand Safety Checklist

Before approving any influencer partnership:

```
□ Review last 3 months of content for controversial statements
□ Check for competitor partnerships (conflict of interest?)
□ Verify audience demographics match our ICP
□ Confirm no history of fake followers/engagement
□ Check for FTC compliance track record
□ Review comments/community for toxicity
□ Verify they actually use AI tools (authentic interest, not just money)
```

## Scaling the Influencer Program

### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1-3)
- Partner with 5-10 micro influencers (Tier 3)
- Focus on YouTube and Twitter/X
- Test different content formats (dedicated, integration, review)
- Establish affiliate program infrastructure
- Budget: $2,000-$5,000/month

### Phase 2: Growth (Month 4-6)
- Scale to 15-25 active influencer partnerships
- Add mid-tier influencers (Tier 2)
- Launch newsletter sponsorships
- Expand to developer influencers for Stone AI Tools
- Budget: $5,000-$15,000/month

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 7-12)
- 30-50 active partnerships across all tiers
- Dedicated influencer manager (part-time or agency)
- Always-on affiliate program with self-serve signup
- Influencer events (virtual meetups, early access)
- Budget: $10,000-$30,000/month

### Phase 4: Ecosystem (Month 13+)
- Influencer advisory board (top 5 influencers give product feedback)
- Co-created products or features (influencer-branded agent configurations)
- Annual influencer summit
- Influencer-generated case studies and testimonials
- Budget: Scaled to ROAS targets
