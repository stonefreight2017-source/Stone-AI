## Email Copy Fundamentals

### The Email Copy Hierarchy

Every email has four layers that determine whether it gets read and acted on:

1. **Subject line** — Determines open rate (40% of the battle)
2. **Preview text** — Supports the subject, visible in inbox (20%)
3. **First line** — Determines if they keep reading (25%)
4. **CTA** — Determines if they act (15%)

If ANY layer fails, the whole email fails. You cannot compensate for a bad subject line with brilliant body copy — they'll never see it.

### Email Copy Rules

1. **One email = one goal.** Never ask for two things. The reader should know exactly what you want them to do.
2. **Write the CTA first.** Then work backward. Every sentence should push toward that one action.
3. **Subject line = headline.** Apply the same headline formulas from landing page copy.
4. **Personalization is not a name.** "Hey {first_name}" is table stakes. Real personalization references their behavior, their tier, their usage.
5. **Short paragraphs only.** 1-3 sentences max per paragraph. Email is scanned, not read.
6. **One link, repeated.** The same CTA link should appear 2-3 times. Different anchor text, same destination.
7. **Mobile first.** 67% of emails are opened on mobile. Subject line must work at 40 characters.

## Subject Line Formulas

### The 15 Proven Formulas

1. **The Curiosity Gap**: "Most people miss this about their AI tools"
2. **The Number**: "3 agents you're not using (but should be)"
3. **The Personal**: "{first_name}, quick question about your workflow"
4. **The How-To**: "How to save 10 hours this week with AI agents"
5. **The Fear**: "Your data expires in 30 days"
6. **The Social Proof**: "Why 2,000+ founders switched to Stone AI"
7. **The Question**: "Are you still using 5 different AI tools?"
8. **The Direct**: "Your upgrade is ready"
9. **The Scarcity**: "50% off — this week only"
10. **The News**: "New: 3 agents just dropped for {plan_name} users"
11. **The Story**: "How a solo founder replaced a 5-person team"
12. **The Contrast**: "Free plan vs Starter: what {amount} actually buys you"
13. **The Mistake**: "The #1 mistake founders make with AI tools"
14. **The Compliment**: "You've completed {count} tasks — you're in the top 10%"
15. **The Breakup**: "Should I stop emailing you?"

### Subject Line Rules

1. **Under 40 characters** — truncation kills open rates
2. **No ALL CAPS** — spam filter trigger
3. **No exclamation marks** — looks desperate
4. **Personalization tokens work** — {first_name} in subject adds 2-5% to opens
5. **Emoji: test it** — works for some audiences, hurts others. A/B test before committing
6. **Preview text is mandatory** — don't let the email client choose for you
7. **A/B test every send** — minimum two subject lines per campaign

### Subject Line A/B Testing

**What to test**:
- Curiosity vs direct
- Short (3-5 words) vs descriptive (8-12 words)
- With personalization vs without
- Question vs statement
- Emoji vs no emoji

**How to test**:
- 20% of list gets variant A, 20% gets variant B
- Wait 2 hours for opens to accumulate
- Winner goes to remaining 60%
- Minimum 1,000 recipients per variant for significance

## Personalization Tokens

### Basic Tokens (Everyone Uses These)

| Token | Example | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| {first_name} | "Hey Sarah" | Low (expected) |
| {company_name} | "How's DataFlow going?" | Medium |
| {plan_name} | "Your Starter plan" | Medium |

### Behavioral Tokens (What Actually Works)

| Token | Example | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| {most_used_agent} | "Your Copywriter agent has been busy" | High |
| {task_count} | "47 tasks completed this month" | High |
| {days_active} | "You've been with us for 23 days" | Medium |
| {unused_agents} | "You haven't tried Data Analyst yet" | High |
| {last_login_days} | "It's been 5 days since you logged in" | Medium |
| {recent_feature} | "The feature you requested just shipped" | Very High |

### Dynamic Content Blocks

Beyond tokens, entire sections can be personalized based on:

1. **Tier**: Free users see upgrade content, paid users see feature depth
2. **Usage pattern**: Heavy users see advanced tips, light users see basics
3. **Industry**: Adjust examples and case studies to match their business type
4. **Lifecycle stage**: New users get onboarding, mature users get retention
5. **Engagement history**: Openers get longer emails, non-openers get shorter/punchier

### Personalization Anti-Patterns

- **Don't over-personalize** — it becomes creepy. "I see you logged in 14 times last Tuesday" is too much.
- **Don't personalize wrong** — "{first_name}" rendering as "null" or the wrong name is worse than no personalization.
- **Don't fake personalization** — "I was just thinking about your business" when you have no context = dishonest.
- **Don't personalize the unimportant** — Personalizing the footer doesn't move the needle. Focus on subject, first line, and CTA.

## Email Design and Formatting Rules

### Layout Principles

1. **Maximum width**: 600px (email standard)
2. **Single column for mobile**: No side-by-side content
3. **Font size**: 16px minimum body, 14px minimum fine print
4. **Line height**: 1.5x minimum
5. **Paragraph spacing**: Double space between paragraphs
6. **CTA button size**: Minimum 44x44px tap target (mobile)

### Plain Text vs HTML

**Use plain text for**:
- Founder/CEO emails (feels personal)
- 1:1 outreach
- Re-engagement (less "marketing" feel)
- Churn prevention (authentic > polished)

**Use HTML for**:
- Feature announcements (screenshots help)
- Product updates (formatting aids scanning)
- Marketing campaigns (brand consistency)
- Welcome emails (design reinforces first impression)

### Email Length by Type

| Email Type | Ideal Length | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | 150-200 words | 250 words |
| Feature education | 150-250 words | 300 words |
| Upgrade nudge | 100-200 words | 250 words |
| Churn prevention | 100-150 words | 200 words |
| Re-engagement | 100-150 words | 200 words |
| Cold outreach | 75-125 words | 150 words |
| Transactional | 50-100 words | 150 words |

## Measurement and Optimization

### Email Benchmarks for SaaS

| Metric | Good | Great | Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 20-25% | 25-35% | 35%+ |
| Click rate | 2-3% | 3-5% | 5%+ |
| Click-to-open rate | 10-15% | 15-25% | 25%+ |
| Unsubscribe rate | < 0.5% | < 0.3% | < 0.1% |
| Reply rate (outreach) | 1-3% | 3-5% | 5%+ |
| Conversion rate | 0.5-1% | 1-3% | 3%+ |

### What to Optimize When

| Problem | Metric to Watch | What to Change |
|---|---|---|
| Low opens | Open rate | Subject line, send time, sender name |
| Opens but no clicks | Click rate | Body copy, CTA, relevance |
| Clicks but no conversion | Conversion rate | Landing page, offer, friction |
| High unsubscribes | Unsub rate | Frequency, relevance, segmentation |
| Spam complaints | Complaint rate | Permission, expectations, frequency |

### Send Time Optimization

**General SaaS benchmarks**:
- Tuesday-Thursday: Highest open rates
- 10:00 AM recipient's timezone: Peak
- Avoid Monday mornings (inbox overload) and Friday afternoons (checked out)

**Better approach**: Use send-time optimization based on individual open history. When did THIS person last open an email? Send the next one at that time.

## Legal and Deliverability

### CAN-SPAM / GDPR Compliance

1. **Physical address required** in every commercial email
2. **Unsubscribe link required** — must work within 10 days (CAN-SPAM), immediately (best practice)
3. **No purchased lists** — ever
4. **Consent**: Explicit opt-in for marketing emails (GDPR), implied for transactional
5. **Sender identification**: "From" name and email must be recognizable
6. **Subject line accuracy**: Subject must relate to email content (no bait-and-switch)

### Deliverability Checklist

- [ ] SPF, DKIM, DMARC records configured for sending domain
- [ ] Dedicated sending domain (not the main business domain)
- [ ] Warm-up plan for new sending domains (start with 50/day, scale over 4 weeks)
- [ ] Clean list regularly (remove bounces, complainers, 6-month inactive)
- [ ] Monitor sender reputation (Google Postmaster Tools, Sender Score)
- [ ] Text-to-image ratio > 80/20 (mostly text)
- [ ] No URL shorteners in email body (spam trigger)
- [ ] Test with Mail-Tester.com before major sends
