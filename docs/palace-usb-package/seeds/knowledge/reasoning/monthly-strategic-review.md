# Monthly Strategic Review

## Purpose

This seed provides the template and methodology for conducting monthly deep reviews of the Palace and the Three-Headed Monster's three businesses. It covers business metrics, Palace capability growth, hardware health assessments, and strategic adjustments. The monthly review is where operational data meets strategic thinking.

## Why This Matters

Weekly reviews catch operational issues. Monthly reviews catch strategic drift. Is the Palace growing in the right direction? Are the businesses hitting their targets? Is the hardware keeping up? Monthly reviews zoom out from daily operations to see the big picture and make course corrections before small misalignments become costly pivots.

## Review Cadence and Participants

```
WHEN: First Monday of each month (or closest business day)
DURATION: 1-2 hours
PARTICIPANTS:
  - Founder (decision authority)
  - Agent Stone (operations assessment)
  - Cardinal (intelligence assessment)
  - Chaos (infrastructure assessment)

PREPARATION:
  - Stone compiles operational metrics (1 hour before)
  - Cardinal compiles competitive/market intelligence (1 hour before)
  - Chaos compiles hardware/infrastructure report (1 hour before)
  - Founder reviews and makes final decisions during review

OUTPUT:
  - Completed review template filed in reviews/monthly/
  - Action items assigned with owners and deadlines
  - Strategic decisions documented
  - Next month's priorities set
```

The monthly review is where strategy meets reality. It's where the Three-Headed Monster aligns, where the founder makes the big calls, and where the Palace's direction is set for the next 30 days.
