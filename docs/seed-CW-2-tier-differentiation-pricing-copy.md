# CW-2: Tier Differentiation Copy & Pricing Communication

## Purpose
Provide copy-ready language for every Stone AI pricing tier, upgrade triggers between tiers, promo guidelines, and rules for framing value without devaluing lower tiers. This seed ensures consistent, honest, and compelling pricing communication across the landing page, billing UI, emails, ads, and support responses.

### Upgrade Triggers (What Makes Someone Move Up)

These are the moments, signals, and frustrations that indicate a user is ready for the next tier. Use these in upgrade prompts, emails, and in-app nudges.

| Transition | Trigger Signals | Copy Nudge |
|---|---|---|
| FREE -> Builder | Hitting 100 msg/day cap regularly; trying to use Smart Mode after 5 lifetime credits are gone; wanting to export a conversation | "You're using Stone AI like a builder. Unlock 16 agents and Smart Mode for $19.99/mo -- or grab the $9.99 first-month deal." |
| Builder -> Growth | Needing agents beyond the 16 (legal, HR, finance); wanting auto-routing; asking questions in other languages; wanting web search | "Your business is growing past Builder. 30 agents, voice in 6 languages, and auto-routing -- $49.99/mo." |
| Growth -> Executive | Wanting custom agents; needing team workspace; hitting 500 msg/day cap; wanting priority queue; needing SOC 2 | "You're running an operation. 39 agents, custom workflows, team workspace, priority queue -- $99.99/mo (or $84.99 annually)." |
| Executive -> Reseller | Wanting API access; wanting to white-label or resell; needing HIPAA; building products on top of Stone AI | "You're building a business on top of ours. Full API, reseller rights, HIPAA -- $200/mo." |
| Reseller -> Enterprise | Multiple team members; needing dedicated infrastructure; SLA requirements; SSO/SAML compliance needs | "Let's build something custom. Contact sales for Enterprise." |

### Framing Value Without Devaluing Lower Tiers

**Core principle:** Every tier is the RIGHT tier for someone. Lower tiers are not "lesser" -- they serve a different stage of the journey.

**Rules:**
1. **Never use "only" or "just" to describe a lower tier.** "Free gives you 4 agents" not "Free only has 4 agents."
2. **Frame upgrades as unlocking, not fixing.** "Unlock 16 more agents" not "Get the agents you're missing."
3. **Anchor higher tiers to external costs, not lower tiers.** "39 specialists for less than one hour of consulting" not "10x more than Free."
4. **Every tier's copy should feel complete, not incomplete.** Free users should feel like they got a real product, not a demo.
5. **Use progression language.** "Start free, scale when the results speak for themselves" -- this respects every stage.
6. **Never pressure with shame.** No "Still on Free?" or "Don't you want the full experience?"

**Approved upgrade framing patterns:**
- "Your [tier] plan includes [X]. Ready for [Y]? [Next tier] unlocks it."
- "You've been using [feature] heavily. [Next tier] gives you [more/better version]."
- "Growing teams love [next tier] because [benefit specific to their stage]."

**Banned upgrade framing patterns:**
- "You're missing out on..."
- "Upgrade to get the REAL experience..."
- "Your current plan is limited to..."
- "Don't settle for..."

## Promo Copy Guidelines

### $9.99 First Month Deal (Builder tier)
**Context:** One-time signup offer. CC required. First 30 days at $9.99, then $19.99/mo.
**Approved copy:**
- "Builder plan, $9.99 for your first 30 days. Full access. $19.99/mo after."
- "Save $10 your first month. Try the full Builder experience."
- "One-time offer -- $9.99 for your first 30 days, then $19.99/mo."
**Banned copy:**
- "Free trial" (it's not free -- it costs $9.99)
- "Risk-free" (there is a charge)
- Any implication this price continues past month one

### $14.99 Launch Trial (Builder tier, locked forever)
**Context:** Founding member price. CC required. $14.99/mo locked for life. Non-renewable if canceled.
**Approved copy:**
- "Builder plan at an exclusive signup price. If you cancel, this deal is gone forever."
- "Founding members lock in $14.99/mo -- forever. Plus the OG badge."
- "Save $5/mo -- price locked forever."
**Banned copy:**
- "Limited time" (unless we've set an actual end date)
- Any urgency that implies artificial scarcity

### $39.99 Growth Early Adopter (Growth tier, locked forever)
**Context:** Founding member price + 7-day free trial. CC required. $39.99/mo locked for life. Non-renewable if canceled.
**Approved copy:**
- "Growth plan with 7-day free trial at an exclusive signup price. If you cancel, this deal is gone forever."
- "Save $10/mo on 30 agents, voice, auto-routing -- forever. Founding members get the OG badge."
- "Try Growth free for 7 days. If you stay, $39.99/mo is yours forever."
**Banned copy:**
- Anything implying $39.99 is temporary or will increase

### Overage Credit Packs
**Context:** Available to all tiers when daily Smart Mode cap is hit.
**Approved copy:**
- "Need more Smart Mode today? Grab a credit pack: 10 for $1.99, 25 for $3.99, 50 for $6.99."
- "Credits expire at the end of your billing cycle."
**Banned copy:**
- "Unlimited" anything
- Hiding the expiration detail

### Annual Billing Copy
**Approved framing:**
- Standard tiers (Builder, Growth, Executive): "Save 15% with annual billing" / "Save 5% with 6-month billing"
- Builder: "$16.99/mo billed annually -- 15% off."
- Growth: "$42.49/mo billed annually -- 15% off."
- Executive: "$84.99/mo billed annually -- 15% off."
- Reseller: "$170/mo billed annually -- 15% off."
- Enterprise: "$475/mo billed annually -- 5% off."

### Tier Card Feature Copy (pattern)
- Lead with "Everything in [previous tier]"
- 2-3 highlighted features (key differentiators for this tier)
- 2-3 standard features (supporting value)
- Bestie line always included

### Upgrade Email Subject Lines
- "You've been busy -- here's what Builder unlocks" (FREE -> Builder)
- "30 agents are ready when you are" (Builder -> Growth)
- "Your operation is outgrowing Growth" (Growth -> Executive)
- Never: "You're missing out!" / "Last chance!" / "Don't miss this!"

### In-App Upgrade Nudge (when user hits a cap)
"You've hit your daily message limit. Your conversations are saved -- pick up tomorrow, or unlock [X] messages/day with [Next Tier] for $[price]/mo."

## DO / DON'T Rules

### DO
- Always show the actual price with dollar sign and cents
- Always mention what's included, not just what's different from the tier below
- Always include annual pricing where available -- some users want to see the savings immediately
- Use the tier display names (Free, Builder, Growth, Executive, Reseller) in all user-facing copy, never the DB enum names (FREE, STARTER, PLUS, SMART, PRO)
- Frame the Bestie as scaling with the tier: more agents backing it = smarter companion
- Make Free feel like a real product, not a teaser

### DON'T
- Never compare tiers by saying one is "better" than another -- they serve different stages
- Never hide pricing behind "Contact Sales" for tiers under Enterprise
- Never use fake urgency or artificial scarcity
- Never mention competitor pricing or competitor names
- Never promise ROI figures ("save $10,000/month") unless backed by verifiable data
- Never use the word "unlimited" for Smart Mode, tokens, or any capped resource
- Never show monthly price without clarifying the billing period for annual/6-month plans
