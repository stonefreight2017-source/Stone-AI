SECTION 1 — CRITICAL: MUST COMPLETE BEFORE MARCH 14

Business Formation

Form an LLC
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 1-2 hours Cost: $100-$200 DIY
Without an LLC, you are personally liable for every lawsuit and every dollar. A single user claim over AI advice gone wrong could take your personal assets. File via NY DOS (dos.ny.gov) or Wyoming Secretary of State ($100 + $60/yr registered agent). NY requires LLC publication in newspaper(s) within 120 days of formation — cost varies $300-$1,500 by county.
Obtain an EIN (Federal Tax ID)
CRITICAL Time: 10 minutes Cost: Free DIY
IRS.gov — instant online issuance. Required for business bank account, Stripe, and tax filing.
Open a business bank account
CRITICAL Time: 1 hour Cost: Free DIY
Stripe payouts must go to a business account. Commingling personal and business funds pierces your LLC veil — meaning you lose the liability protection you just paid for. Mercury, Relay, or Chase Business are good options.
Get a PO Box or virtual mailing address
CRITICAL Time: 30 minutes Cost: $50-$150/year DIY
CAN-SPAM Act requires a physical postal address in all marketing emails. If you work from home and don't want to publish your home address, get a PO Box or virtual mailbox (iPostal1, Earth Class Mail). This address can also serve as your LLC registered agent address.

Terms of Service Fixes

Fix governing law clause — change to "State of New York" explicitly
CRITICAL Time: 15 minutes Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Current ToS Section 19 says "the state where Stone AI is headquartered" — this is vague. Courts want specificity. Change to "the State of New York" in both Section 19 (governing law) and Section 17 (arbitration location).
Add NY auto-renewal compliance language (GBL Section 527-a)
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 30 minutes Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
NY law requires: (1) clear and conspicuous disclosure of auto-renewal terms BEFORE purchase, (2) affirmative consent to auto-renewal, (3) easy cancellation mechanism, (4) confirmation after purchase including auto-renewal terms. NY AG actively enforces — fines of $500-$10,000 per violation.
Add auto-renewal disclosure to the checkout UI
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 1-2 hours Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Before the purchase button, display: "This subscription will automatically renew every [period] and your payment method will be charged [price] unless you cancel before the renewal date. You may cancel at any time through your Billing settings." Must be "clear and conspicuous" — not fine print.
Add promotional pricing disclosures
CRITICAL Time: 1 hour Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
The $9.99 first month, $14.99 trial, and $39.99 growth promotions must clearly disclose: (1) the promotional period, (2) the regular price after promotion ends, (3) that the subscription auto-renews at the regular price. NY auto-renewal law applies to promotional-to-regular price transitions.

Privacy Policy Fixes

Add NY SHIELD Act compliance section
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 30 minutes Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Add a section stating your data security program and breach notification commitment. NY SHIELD Act requires notification "in the most expedient time possible" after a breach. If 500+ NY residents affected, also notify the AG within 10 days.
Add "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link
CRITICAL Time: 1 hour Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
CCPA/CPRA requires a visible link — not just language buried in the privacy policy. Since you serve ads (AdSense on free tier), this is arguably "sharing" personal information. Add a visible link in the site footer and in user settings. California AG actively enforces.
Add Vercel, Neon, and Cloudflare as sub-processors
CRITICAL Time: 15 minutes Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Privacy Policy Section 9 lists Clerk, Stripe, OpenAI, and Google AdSense but omits Vercel (hosting — sees all traffic), Neon (database — stores all data), and Cloudflare (CDN/DNS — sees all traffic). These are material sub-processors handling personal data and must be disclosed.

AI-Specific Compliance

Add agent-specific disclaimers for high-risk agents
CRITICAL Time: 1-2 hours Cost: Free (code edit) DIY (lawyer review recommended)
Display at point of use (not just in ToS): Health/Wellness agent — "Not medical advice. Not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment." Legal-related agents — "Not legal advice. Does not create a lawyer-client relationship." Financial agents — "Not financial advice. Not a registered investment advisor." If someone follows your Health agent's advice and is harmed, they will sue.
Review all marketing copy for FTC compliance
CRITICAL Time: 1 hour Cost: Free DIY
Verify: no false AI capability claims, "free tier" is genuinely free, promotional pricing clearly shows regular price, "local-first" claims are accurate. Brand sweep already completed March 7 — verify no new issues introduced.

Tax Registration

Register for NY sales tax (Certificate of Authority)
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 30 minutes Cost: Free DIY
New York taxes SaaS as tangible personal property (TB-ST-128). If you sell to NY customers and don't collect sales tax, YOU owe it personally. Register via NY Department of Taxation and Finance online portal. Consider enabling Stripe Tax ($0.50/transaction) to automate multi-state collection.

Internal Documentation

Document your data security program (even 1 page)
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 1 hour Cost: Free DIY
NY SHIELD Act requires businesses holding NY residents' data to maintain "reasonable safeguards." Your technical safeguards are solid (AES-256-GCM, TLS, rate limiting, audit logging). What's missing is a documented program mapping your safeguards to the SHIELD Act's three categories: administrative, technical, and physical. This is what you'd show a regulator. Small business exception applies if under 50 employees, under $3M revenue, and under 5,000 records.
Create a breach response plan (1 page)
CRITICAL NY-SPECIFIC Time: 30 minutes Cost: Free DIY
Procedure: (1) Detect breach, (2) Assess scope, (3) Notify affected NY residents "in the most expedient time possible," (4) Notify NY AG, Division of State Police, and Department of State if 5,000+ NYers affected. Have this ready internally — you don't need to publish it.

SECTION 2 — HIGH PRIORITY: COMPLETE WITHIN 30 DAYS (by April 14)

File trademarks: "Stone AI," "Best AI," "Stone AI Tools," "Concept E"
HIGH Cost: ~$2,100 (4 marks) DIY possible, lawyer recommended
File in Class 42 (SaaS) and Class 9 (downloadable software). Do a knockout search on USPTO TESS first. TEAS Plus filing: $250/class. Consider Class 35 for the tools directory. Attorney-assisted: $500-$800 per mark.
Implement cookie consent banner
HIGH Cost: Free (open-source) to $50/mo (CookieYes, Termly) DIY
No cookie banner exists. EU ePrivacy Directive requires opt-in consent for non-essential cookies. Even in the US, having a cookie notice is best practice given AdSense usage on free tier.
Add GDPR-specific section to Privacy Policy
HIGH Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Add: legal basis for processing, right to lodge complaint, data transfer mechanisms (SCCs), right to data portability. Geo-compliance system handles EU consent programmatically, but the policy text is incomplete. GDPR enforcement against small US SaaS is rare but the text costs nothing to add.
Obtain E&O (Professional Liability) Insurance
HIGH Cost: $500-$1,500/year DIY (Next Insurance, Hiscox)
The most important insurance for an AI SaaS. Covers claims that your service caused harm through errors, omissions, or negligent advice. If someone sues because your Health agent gave bad advice, E&O is what protects you. Disclaimers are your first line of defense. E&O is your second.
Obtain General Liability Insurance
HIGH Cost: $300-$600/year DIY (same providers)
Covers third-party bodily injury, property damage, advertising injury claims. Some enterprise customers will require proof of insurance before signing.
Draft LLC Operating Agreement
HIGH NY-SPECIFIC Cost: Free (template) to $500 (lawyer) DIY
NY LLC Act Section 417 requires an operating agreement even for single-member LLCs. LegalZoom, Stripe Atlas, or Northwest have free templates.
Audit open-source license compliance
HIGH Cost: Free DIY
Run: npx license-checker --production --failOn "GPL" — GPL/AGPL licenses are "viral" and could require you to open-source your code. Your main dependencies (Next.js MIT, Prisma Apache 2.0) are fine, but check ALL transitive dependencies.
Run basic WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility audit
HIGH NY-SPECIFIC Cost: Free DIY
NY is the number one state for web accessibility lawsuits — 2,000+ ADA suits in 2023-2024. Run Lighthouse accessibility audit, axe DevTools, and WAVE. Check: alt text on images, form labels, color contrast (zinc-950 bg with zinc-400 text may fail), keyboard navigation. Only 18 aria-labels found across the entire codebase — this needs attention.
Add "Dispute Resolution: Small Claims Exception" to ToS
HIGH Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Courts frequently strike down arbitration clauses without a small claims carve-out. Add: "Either party may bring individual claims in small claims court if they qualify."
Add Electronic Communications Consent clause to ToS
HIGH Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Required under E-SIGN Act. Users must consent to receiving disclosures, notices, and agreements electronically.
Verify NYC Local Law 144 does not apply
HIGH NY-SPECIFIC Cost: Free (research) DIY
LL144 regulates "automated employment decision tools." Your Career Coach and Resume agents could be argued to be AEDTs if used in hiring decisions. Add disclaimer to HR-related agents: "This tool is not an automated employment decision tool under NYC Local Law 144. Output is for informational purposes only."
Set up quarterly estimated tax payments
HIGH Cost: Varies CPA recommended ($200-$500 initial setup)
First payment due April 15, 2026. You'll owe: federal self-employment tax (15.3%), federal income tax, NY state tax (4-10.9%), NYC tax (3.078-3.876% if NYC resident), and potentially NYC Unincorporated Business Tax (4% on net income over $95K). Consider S-Corp election when revenue exceeds $40K/yr.
Prepare written contractor agreement template
HIGH NY-SPECIFIC Cost: Free (template) DIY
NY Freelance Isn't Free Act requires written contracts for any engagement worth $800+. Penalties for non-compliance: double damages plus attorneys' fees. Have a template ready before engaging any contractor.

SECTION 3 — MEDIUM PRIORITY: COMPLETE WITHIN 90 DAYS (by June 14)

Obtain Cyber Liability Insurance
MEDIUM Cost: $500-$2,000/year DIY (Coalition, At-Bay, Corvus)
Covers data breach costs: notification, forensics, legal defense, credit monitoring for affected users. Given you store conversation data, personality data, and email addresses, a breach could be expensive.
Register copyright for core platform code
MEDIUM Cost: $65 per registration DIY (US Copyright Office)
Copyright is automatic, but registration is required to sue for statutory damages ($150K per infringement vs. actual damages only). Register as a literary work.
Analyze multi-state sales tax nexus
MEDIUM Cost: Free (research) or $50-$200/mo (TaxJar, Stripe Tax) DIY or automated
Once you have customers in multiple states, economic nexus triggers (typically $100K sales or 200 transactions). States that tax SaaS include NY, TX, PA, WA, CT, and others. Stripe Tax automates collection.
Create enterprise Data Processing Agreement template
MEDIUM Cost: Free (template) to $1,000 (lawyer) Lawyer recommended
Enterprise customers will request a DPA before signing. Have a template ready.
Full GDPR compliance review (if EU traction grows)
MEDIUM Cost: $1,000-$3,000 (lawyer) Lawyer recommended
Engage CPA for tax optimization and S-Corp election analysis
MEDIUM Cost: $200-$500 initial + $500-$1,500 annual
S-Corp election can save significant self-employment tax once revenue exceeds $40K/yr. A CPA will more than pay for themselves in tax savings.
Add accessibility statement page
MEDIUM Cost: Free (code edit) DIY
Standard practice: /accessibility page stating commitment to WCAG 2.1 AA, known issues, and how to report problems. Provides good faith evidence if sued.
Verify data disposal procedures work as documented
MEDIUM Cost: Free DIY
ToS says data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Verify this actually happens — hard delete, not soft delete. Check that database backups also have retention limits.

Likelihood: LOW. Impact: HIGH — injunction, forced open-source. Fix: Run license audit on all dependencies.
